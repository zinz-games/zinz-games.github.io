WordCurio privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Android game WordCurio (package com.wordcurio.game) handles, why, and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible

The game is made by ZinZ. For any privacy question, write to andreaog@gmail.com.

2. In short

  • You don't need an account, and the game never asks for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored only on your phone. The developer has no servers and never receives it.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Optional purchases go through Google Play. The developer never sees your payment details.
  • The game also works offline: then it shows no ads and sends no data.

3. Data stored on your phone

The game stores on your device: the level you reached, coins, unlocked curious words, your Daily puzzle streak, theme and sound settings, how many reward videos you watched today, and whether you bought "Remove ads". This data is not sent to anyone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or use Settings → Erase progress (the "Remove ads" purchase stays linked to your Google Play account).

4. Third-party services

Some features use outside services, which handle data under their own privacy policies:

5. Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google shows a form on first launch so you can choose whether to consent. With consent, ads may be based on your interests; without it, Google may show non-personalised or limited ads. You can review your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy (an internet connection is needed). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

The "Remove ads" purchase removes the ads between levels. Reward videos stay available, but they only play when you choose them.

6. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Consent (GDPR art. 6.1.a) for ads based on your interests and for access to device information for advertising.
  • Performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b) for purchases you request.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f) for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and technical operation, within the limits set by Google and Unity.

7. International transfers

Google and Unity may process data outside your country, for example in the United States, with the safeguards required by law (such as standard contractual clauses or the EU–US and UK–US data frameworks).

8. How long data is kept

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the game or erase your progress. Data handled by Google and Unity is kept for the periods stated in their policies.

9. Age

The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (in Settings → Privacy). Because the developer receives no data that identifies you, for data handled by Google and Unity you can also contact them directly. You can write to andreaog@gmail.com, and you have the right to complain to your data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK).

US residents: the developer does not sell or share your personal information. Ad-related data is handled by Google as described above; you can limit ad tracking by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
